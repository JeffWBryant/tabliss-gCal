# Privacy Policy **Last updated: August 27, 2026** This privacy policy explains how the Tabliss gCal widget handles information when you connect it to your Google account. ## Information Accessed When you choose to connect Google Calendar and Google Tasks, the widget requests permission to access your Google Calendar and Google Tasks data. The widget uses this access to display your calendar events and tasks within the Tabliss widget. ## How Your Information Is Used Your Google Calendar and Google Tasks information is used solely to provide the functionality of the widget. The widget does not sell, rent, or share your Google Calendar or Google Tasks information with third parties. ## Data Storage The widget does not store your Google Calendar or Google Tasks data on a server. Authentication is performed through Google's authentication services, and the widget uses the access granted by Google to retrieve your data directly from Google's APIs. ## Third-Party Services This widget uses Google APIs, including: * Google Calendar API * Google Tasks API * Google Identity Services Your use of Google services is also subject to Google's privacy policy and terms of service. ## Revoking Access You can revoke the widget's access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account security settings. After access is revoked, the widget will no longer be able to retrieve your Google Calendar or Google Tasks information. ## Contact For questions about this application or this privacy policy, please contact the repository owner through the GitHub repository hosting this application.
